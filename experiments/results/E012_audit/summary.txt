How much is a player's Combine worth once he starts playing? We measured it in games of his own NFL tracking. For 12 game traits we estimated how noisy one game is and how well the Combine predicts a player's long-run level, and converted the two into Combine Game Equivalents. Where the Combine helps at all, it is worth about half a game of a receiver's top-speed tracking and about one game of a defensive lineman's first-second burst. For most traits it is worth no measurable games. Combine tracking adds essentially nothing beyond the stopwatch, measurements and draft slot, and it did not improve forecasts for 2025 rookies. A rule that blends the Combine-based estimate with a player's own games, frozen before the 2025 season, cut rookies' forecast error by 72% after one game, mostly by not overreacting to it. For 9 of 12 traits, his own games outweigh the prior within two to seven games.
