## House style (applies to every item)

- **Palette.** Keep the E008 convention:
  - blue = the target is a game movement trait;
  - orange = the target is a game outcome;
  - grey = robustness and other context.
  - Check color-blind safety with the `dataviz` skill validator before final render.
- **Sign convention.** Partial r is always signed so that right/up means the preregistered direction.
- **Uncertainty.** Always show a 95% CI. Where a null is shown, mark the one-sided 80%-power detectable effect (MDE) as a tick. The primary estimate and its CI are always the most salient marks; post hoc corrections are drawn lighter and thinner.
- **Words.** Plain-English row labels (no E-numbers on the figure; put them in the notebook). Titles state the finding, not the method. Say "scouting baseline", not "B2".
- **Labels.** Every post hoc element is labeled "post hoc" on the figure itself.
- **Output.**
  - Static figures: PNG at 2x.
  - The animation: GIF, at most about 5 MB, with a still fallback frame.
  - Alt text for each, kept in the notebook.
- **Captions.** At most 20 words each. They count toward the 2,000 words. The v1 captions total 116 words (Table 1 caption included).
